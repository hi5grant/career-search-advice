# Prompt: Generate a Targeted Resume

Target company: [COMPANY]
Target role: [ROLE]
Job posting: [URL]

Read repository instructions, the master career inventory, job posting, and company match assessment.

Before drafting:
1. Identify the most relevant evidence.
2. List material uncertainties and ask me specific questions.
3. Wait for answers to those questions.
4. Omit claims I do not confirm.

Then create an ATS-readable resume tailored to this role. Keep claims factual and interview-defensible. Distinguish my personal contribution from team outcomes, technical leadership from formal people management, architecture influence from final authority, and personal projects from employment.

Do not keyword-stuff, invent metrics, inflate titles, include internal notes, or modify the canonical inventory to fit the posting. Save the final resume in generated-resumes/ using a clear company-role-date filename.
