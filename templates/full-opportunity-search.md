# Prompt: Evaluate a Company and Role

I am evaluating this opportunity. Use my private career-tools repository as the source of truth.

Company: [COMPANY]
Role: [ROLE]
Job posting URL: [URL]

## Instructions

1. Read repository instructions and the master career inventory first.
2. Research the company using current, credible public sources.
3. Separate company-reported facts, filings, third-party estimates, and analysis.
4. Evaluate business model, revenue, profitability/cash, funding, competition, strategy, hiring trends, and stability. Clearly state what is unavailable.
5. Analyze the job posting and save its URL and a structured summary.
6. Compare requirements with the inventory as direct matches, transferable/adjacent experience, gaps, and unknowns.
7. Identify questions that need my answer before drafting.
8. Provide a reasoned role/company assessment and explain any score; do not let a numeric score hide deal-breakers.
9. Save records in companies/<company-slug>/.
10. Do not alter the master inventory to fit this role.

Never invent financials, recruiter identities, experience, or metrics. Cite public research sources in the company record.
